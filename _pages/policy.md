This website shares Divya Akula’s articles, books, and speaking information.

## Website analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use its pages. Google Analytics may use cookies and collect usage information, such as pages visited and device or browser information. See [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information about how Google handles data.

## Comments

Article comments use Giscus, which connects to GitHub Discussions. Signing in and posting a comment is handled through GitHub. See the [Giscus privacy policy](https://github.com/giscus/giscus/blob/main/PRIVACY-POLICY.md) and [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Contact and subscriptions

Contact links open your email application. Information you choose to send by email is used to respond to your enquiry. Newsletter links take you to Substack, where its own privacy policy and subscription settings apply.

## External links

Links to Amazon, YouTube, social networks, and other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.
