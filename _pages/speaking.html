Speaker bio

I’m Divya Akula, an AI and Responsible AI Architect, author, speaker, and community organizer. With over 16 years of experience across the Microsoft ecosystem, I work with organizations to turn emerging technologies into practical solutions that people can use and trust.

My work spans Microsoft Copilot, AI agents, Power Platform, and Responsible AI. As a Senior Technical Consultant at Sulava MEA, I explore not only what technology can do, but also how we can design and use it responsibly.

Recognized as a Microsoft MVP in 2024, I share what I learn through community events, talks, and writing, including my book on Power Apps and OpenAI. I enjoy making complex ideas approachable and bringing people together to learn, experiment, and build with purpose.
